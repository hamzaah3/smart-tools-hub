## 🚀 Pro Tips for Editorial Content

### 1. **Start Strong**
Your first paragraph should hook readers immediately. It's automatically styled larger for emphasis.

### 2. **Use Callout Boxes**
Highlight the most important information in bordered boxes with titles like "NEED TO KNOW", "KEY TAKEAWAYS", or "QUICK FACTS".

### 3. **Bold Important Terms**
Use **bold text** for key concepts, making content scannable.

### 4. **Italicize Names**
Always italicize publication names, movie titles, book titles, etc., like *The New York Times* or *Titanic*.

### 5. **Break Up Long Sections**
Use H3 headings every 3-4 paragraphs to maintain visual interest.

### 6. **Include Lists**
Lists are 3x more likely to be read than paragraphs. Use them liberally.

### 7. **Add Visual Breaks**
Include images, blockquotes, or tables every 400-500 words.

### 8. **Use HR Dividers**
Separate major sections with `---` for a professional double-line divider.
